General Sans (Regular 400, Medium 500, Semibold 600)
--------------------------------------------------
Foundry:  Indian Type Foundry (ITF), via Fontshare — https://fontshare.com
Licence:  ITF Free Font Licence (FFL) 1.0

The ITF Free Font Licence permits use of these fonts, free of charge, for both
personal and commercial projects, including embedding them in a website and
serving them from the site's own domain. The fonts may not be sold on their
own, and the licence text must travel with the font files — which is what this
file is for. Full terms: https://www.fontshare.com/licenses/itf-ffl

Files here are the woff2 web builds, downloaded from Fontshare and served
self-hosted through next/font/local. No third-party request is made at runtime.
